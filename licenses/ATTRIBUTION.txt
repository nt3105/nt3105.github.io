# Local AppIcon assets

These are vector brand marks, not emoji or font-letter substitutes. The application-tile surfaces, reflections and shadows are CSS. Existing destinations remain configured in `script.js`.

| Files | Pinned source | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| telegram.svg | @iconify-json/logos 1.2.16, `telegram` | Original white paper-plane path; remove circular background and crop viewBox for a gradient tile. |
| tiktok.svg | @iconify-json/logos 1.2.16, `tiktok-icon` | Original red/cyan paths; central mark white for the dark tile. |
| c.svg, cpp.svg | @iconify-json/logos 1.2.16, `c`, `c-plusplus` | Original multicolor geometry. |
| html.svg, css.svg | @iconify-json/logos 1.2.16, `html-5`, `css-3` | Original shields; viewBox excludes separate wordmark above shield. |
| javascript.svg, git.svg, gmail.svg, swift.svg | @iconify-json/logos 1.2.16, `javascript`, `git-icon`, `google-gmail-2020`, `swift` | Original geometry and colors. |
| github.svg | simple-icons 13.21.0, `github` | Octocat mark, white fill for a dark tile. |
| facebook.svg | @fortawesome/fontawesome-free 6.7.2, `brands/facebook-f` | Original F mark, white fill. |
| objectivec.svg | devicons/devicon v2.17.0, `objectivec/objectivec-plain.svg` | Original Objective-C mark. |
| email.svg | lucide-static 0.468.0, `mail` | Generic envelope, white stroke. This denotes email, not an invented Apple/Gmail brand logo. |
